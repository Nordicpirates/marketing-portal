Quick to learn

How to play

Three phases per round. There is a how-to-play video for each one.

01 Betting phase

Bluff your crew

Roll your crew dice hidden under the bamboo cup, then bluff on totals. Raise the stakes, lock a number, or call someone a liar, at exactly the right moment.

02 Sail phase

Move the fleet

The round winner rolls the sail die and advances ships 1 to 2 tiles. Battle dice decide naval combat. Tile events keep every move unpredictable.

03 Action phase

Play your hand

Deploy Gold or Silver cards to attack rivals, defend your position, or upgrade your crew. Every card changes the board.
