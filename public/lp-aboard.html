How to play

Each round runs Betting, then Sail, then Action. Same order every time, so new players are bluffing by round two.

01 · Betting phase

Bluff your crew

Roll your crew dice hidden under your cup, then bluff on totals. Raise the stakes, lock a number, or call someone a liar, at exactly the right moment.

02 · Sail phase

Move the fleet

The round winner rolls the sail die and advances ships 1 to 2 tiles. Battle dice decide naval combat. Tile events keep every move unpredictable.

03 · Action phase

Play your hand

Deploy Gold or Silver cards to attack rivals, defend your position, or upgrade your crew. Every card changes the board.
